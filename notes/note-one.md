# Change Your Life as a Coder with these Habits!

## 1. Have Extremely Clear Fundamentals [00:00:42]
* **First Principle Thinking:** Deeply understand the architecture of your projects rather than just copying them. You should be able to figure things out from scratch.
* **System Design & Schema:** Being able to design the database schema for a complex application (like Facebook's friendship graph) shows strong fundamentals beyond basic CRUD operations. 

## 2. Genuinely Like Coding (Interest in CS) [00:02:14]
* Find joy in the art of computer science and the technical aspects of problem-solving.
* You should enjoy training your brain to recognize patterns, debugging independently, and reading documentation without always relying on AI.
* This passion can be acquired over time, even if you didn't initially start in tech.

## 3. The Ability to Sit for Long Hours [00:04:12]
* Top developers can focus for long, uninterrupted sprints (4-5 hours at a time).
* Whether it's for a hackathon or a project deadline, dedicating significant hours (sometimes 10-12 hours a day) helps you build familiarity with your tech stack and accelerates your growth.

## 4. The Power of the Right Peer Group [00:05:31]
* Surround yourself with smart, ambitious, and hardworking peers. If one person in your group gets placed, it often creates a ripple effect where others get placed soon after.
* A good peer group fosters technical growth and healthy competition, much like a leaderboard.

## 5. Keep the Right Optimism (Ignore Market Noise) [00:07:40]
* It's easy to consume negative content about layoffs and the job market, which can lead to a vicious cycle of procrastination and giving up.
* Believe that if you put in the hard work and master your fundamentals, you will remain a highly hirable engineer.

## 6. Take Ownership and Do Smart Outreach [00:08:45]
* **Self-Reliance:** Ultimately, your placement is on you. Don't rely exclusively on campus placements or referrals.
* **Ambitious Projects:** Build specific, complex projects that align with the needs of the companies you want to join.
* **Cold Outreach:** Instead of sending generic AI-generated emails, directly reach out to founders or hiring managers on platforms like X (Twitter) with your impressive projects.

---
**Summary:**
There is no secret formula or magic pill. Success comes from a combination of strong fundamentals, the right peer group, building complex projects, and taking proactive steps to reach out to companies.
