# 5-Day Upper/Lower Workout Plan

**Training days:** Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday  
**Rest days:** Wednesday and Friday

> **Starting workload:** For the first two weeks, perform 2 sets per exercise and only 1 set per exercise on Thursday. Increase toward the listed sets when recovering well.
>
> **Effort:** Finish each set with approximately 2–3 clean repetitions still possible.
>
> **Warm-up:** 5 minutes of easy walking/cycling, followed by light practice sets. Warm-up sets do not count toward the sets below.

## Saturday — Upper A

| Order | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Machine chest press | 3 × 8–12 | 2–3 min |
| 2 | Chest-supported machine row | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min |
| 3 | Lat pulldown | 2 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| 4 | Dumbbell lateral raise | 2 × 12–15 | 60–90 sec |
| 5 | Dumbbell biceps curl | 2 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec |
| 6 | Cable triceps pushdown | 2 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec |

## Sunday — Lower A

**Gym time:** 2:30 PM

| Order | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leg press | 3 × 10–15 | 2–3 min |
| 2 | Seated leg curl | 3 × 10–15 | 90–120 sec |
| 3 | Hip-thrust machine | 2 × 10–15 | 2 min |
| 4 | Standing calf raise | 3 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec |
| 5 | Dead bug | 2 × 6–10 per side | 60 sec |

## Monday — Upper B

**Gym time:** After university

| Order | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incline dumbbell chest press | 3 × 8–12 | 2–3 min |
| 2 | Lat pulldown | 3 × 8–12 | 2 min |
| 3 | Chest-supported machine row | 2 × 10–12 | 2 min |
| 4 | Machine shoulder press | 2 × 8–12 | 2 min |
| 5 | Dumbbell biceps curl | 2 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec |
| 6 | Overhead cable triceps extension | 2 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec |

> Use an incline chest-press machine if needed. Replace overhead triceps extensions with pushdowns if the overhead position is uncomfortable.

## Tuesday — Lower B

**Gym time:** 2:30 PM

| Order | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblet squat | 3 × 8–12 | 2–3 min |
| 2 | Dumbbell Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8–12 | 2–3 min |
| 3 | Leg extension | 2 × 10–15 | 90 sec |
| 4 | Seated leg curl | 2 × 10–15 | 90 sec |
| 5 | Standing calf raise | 3 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec |
| 6 | Forearm plank | 2 × 20–40 seconds | 60 sec |

## Wednesday — Rest

- No weight training.
- Optional easy walking or gentle mobility.

## Thursday — Upper C — Lighter

**Keep approximately 3 clean repetitions in reserve.**

| Order | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Machine chest press | 2 × 10–15 | 2 min |
| 2 | Chest-supported machine row | 2 × 10–15 | 2 min |
| 3 | Dumbbell lateral raise | 2 × 12–20 | 60–90 sec |
| 4 | Reverse pec deck | 2 × 12–20 | 60–90 sec |
| 5 | Dumbbell hammer curl | 2 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec |
| 6 | Cable triceps pushdown | 2 × 10–15 | 60–90 sec |

> If poor sleep leaves you exhausted, replace this session with easy walking and mobility.

## Friday — Rest

- No weight training.
- Optional easy walking or gentle mobility.
