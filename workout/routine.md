# 🎯 Exercise Order Rule

For upper body:

```
Big Chest / Back Exercise
        ↓
Big Chest / Back Exercise
        ↓
Shoulders
        ↓
Rear Delts
        ↓
Biceps
        ↓
Triceps
```

Do not start your workout with biceps or triceps.

Your arms help during your bigger exercises.

Example:

❌ Bad order:

```
Biceps
Triceps
Forearms
Chest Press
Lat Pulldown
```

✅ Better order:

```
Chest
Back
Chest
Back
Shoulders
Biceps
Triceps
```

# 🧠 Muscle Connection

Try to feel the muscle you are training.

## Chest Press

Think:

```
Lower Weight Slowly
      ↓
Feel Chest Stretch
      ↓
Push
      ↓
Squeeze Chest
      ↓
Repeat
```

---

## Lat Pulldown

Don't think:

> Pull the bar with my hands.

Think:

> Bring my elbows down toward my body.

This usually makes it easier to feel your back.

---

## Seated Row

Think:

> Pull my elbows backward and squeeze my back.

Don't just move your hands.

---

## Biceps Curl

```
Curl Up
   ↓
Squeeze Biceps
   ↓
Lower Slowly
```

Avoid swinging your whole body.

---

## Triceps Pushdown

```
Push Down
   ↓
Straighten Arms
   ↓
Squeeze Triceps
   ↓
Return Slowly
```

---

## Leg Extension

Think about squeezing your **quads** at the top.

---

## Leg Curl

Think about squeezing your **hamstrings** when your legs curl.

# 🔒 Should I "Lock" My Muscles?

You may hear people saying:

> "Lock the muscle."

Usually what they mean is:

**contract/squeeze the muscle.**

That's okay.

For example:

Chest Press:

**Press → squeeze chest briefly → lower**

Biceps Curl:

**Curl → squeeze biceps → lower**

But don't violently lock your joints.

### Leg Press

❌ Don't aggressively snap your knees straight.

✅ Get close to straight while staying controlled.

### Chest Press

❌ Don't violently slam your elbows into lockout.

✅ Press with control and keep tension.

# 🏋️ Progressive Overload

This is one of the most important parts of muscle building.

Example:

### Chest Press

Week 1:

```
30 KG
8 reps
8 reps
8 reps
```

Later:

```
30 KG
10 reps
9 reps
9 reps
```

Later:

```
30 KG
12 reps
12 reps
12 reps
```

Now increase the weight.

Example:

```
32.5 KG
8 reps
8 reps
8 reps
```

Then slowly work toward 12 reps again.

---

# 📈 Simple Progression Rule

If your target is:

**3 × 8–12**

Stay at the same weight until you can perform something close to:

```
12
12
12
```

with good form.

Then increase the weight slightly.

---

# ⚠️ Don't Ego Lift

Good example:

```
30 KG
10 clean reps
Good control
Good form
```

Bad example:

```
45 KG
Half reps
Body shaking
Someone helping
Poor control
```

Muscle growth is not about showing people how heavy you can lift.

Focus on:

**Form + Control + Progress**

---

# 🔥 Training Intensity

You don't need to train every set until complete failure.

Usually finish the set while feeling like you could still perform:

**1–3 more clean reps**

Example:

If your absolute maximum is:

**12 reps**

You might stop around:

**9–11 reps**

especially as a beginner.

---

# 🐢 Control Your Reps

A good repetition might look like:

```
Lower Weight = ~2 seconds

Short Stretch

Push / Pull = ~1–2 seconds

Brief Squeeze

Repeat
```

Avoid:

```
Fast Down
Fast Up
Bounce
Repeat
```

Control the weight.

Don't let the weight control you.

# 🍗 Protein

A useful daily target for muscle building is roughly:

**1.6–2.2 grams of protein per KG of bodyweight per day**

Example:

If bodyweight = **70 KG**

```
70 × 1.6 = 112g protein
70 × 2.2 = 154g protein
```

Approximate target:

**112–154g protein/day**

Protein sources:

- Eggs
    
- Chicken
    
- Fish
    
- Beef
    
- Milk
    
- Yogurt
    
- Dal
    
- Beans
    
- Paneer
    
- Soy
    
- Whey Protein
    

Whey is optional.

# ⭐ Main Rules to Remember

1. **Form before weight.**
    
2. **Control every repetition.**
    
3. **Train consistently.**
    
4. **Progress slowly.**
    
5. **Don't change exercises every week.**
    
6. **Keep the same main exercises for around 6–10 weeks.**
    
7. **Eat enough protein.**
    
8. **Sleep 7–9 hours.**
    
9. **Use progressive overload.**
    
10. **Don't compare your weights with other people.**
