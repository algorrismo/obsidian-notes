# Midterm Question Format

**Course:** SOFTWARE REQUIREMENT ENGINEERING [C] [SUMMER 25-26]  
**Total Marks:** 80  
**Time:** 2 Hours

## Exam Structure

- **MCQ and True/False:** 50 × 1 = **50 Marks**
- **Descriptive Questions:** 3 out of 6 → 3 × 10 = **30 Marks**

---

## Possible Short Question Topics

### Lecture 1
- Requirement and its levels with examples
- Non-functional requirements
- Requirement engineering, Requirement Development and Management
- Different phases of requirement development
- Requirement Development and Management boundary
- Baselined Requirements

### Lecture 3
- Vision and Scope document structure
- User classes and different types of user classes with examples
- User persona with example
- Product champion
- Assumed and Implied requirements with examples

### Lecture 4
- Types of Business Rules taxonomy with examples
- Different sections of SRS and their purpose
- Identify flaw in a specification and rewrite the specification correctly

### Lecture 5
- Types of prototypes and their comparison
- MoSCoW technique with example
- Calculate priority of features based on their value%, cost% and risk% from a given table (benefit, penalty, cost and risk assessment)
- Comparison of different informal review techniques
- Formal review technique – Inspection
