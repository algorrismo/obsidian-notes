# Summary of “Survey of Machine Learning Algorithms for Disease Diagnostic”

## Background
This paper by Fatima and Pasha (2017) looks at how machine learning is used in the medical field, specifically for Computer Aided Diagnosis (CAD). Since errors in diagnosing diseases can lead to wrong treatments, the authors wanted to study how different ML algorithms help doctors detect diseases more accurately.

## The Problem
Medical data is huge and complicated, and old statistical models are not good enough to handle missing values or large amounts of categorical data. This makes it hard for hospitals to properly organize and analyze patient data, which can lead to poor diagnosis.

## The Method
The paper compares different machine learning algorithms like Naive Bayes, SVM, Decision Trees, J48, and Neural Networks that other researchers have used for five diseases: heart disease, diabetes, liver disease, dengue, and hepatitis. The purpose was to see which algorithm gives the best accuracy for each disease using tools like WEKA and MATLAB.

## The Results
SVM gave the best accuracy (94.60%) for heart disease. Naive Bayes worked best for diabetes (95%). FT Tree was best for liver disease (97.10%). Rough Set theory hit 100% for dengue, and Feed Forward Neural Network got 98% for hepatitis.

## Conclusion
The paper shows that machine learning genuinely helps improve disease diagnosis accuracy and can support doctors in making faster, better decisions.
