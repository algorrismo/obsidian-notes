# The Golden Rules of Responsible Vibe Coding

1. **Context is King**  
   The better context you give the AI, the better output you get. Invest in context, not shortcuts.

2. **Review Everything**  
   AI is confident but not always correct. Review every output — no exceptions.

3. **Own the Architecture**  
   System design is your responsibility. Do not let the AI decide your architecture.

4. **Document Your Intent**  
   Writing PRDs and specs is part of the workflow, not an optional extra.

5. **Version Control is Non-Negotiable**  
   No matter how fast AI writes code, you cannot go to production without traceability and accountability.

6. **Learn Continuously**  
   Tools and models will change, but concepts and foundation do not get stuck on any single tool.
